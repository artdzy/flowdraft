It needs Apple Intelligence turned on; otherwise the dialog says how to turn it on. The end of the attached recording shows it on a Mac.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
flowdraft is a flowchart editor for people who plan processes: product and operations teams, developers and students mapping a workflow, an automation, a software architecture or a customer conversation. The editor is bundled in the app, opens instantly and works offline; drawings stay on the device. A free account is optional and only adds cloud saving.

3. HOW TO USE
No account is needed for drawing, importing or exporting. For the account features please use the demo account in the Sign-In Information fields.
- The empty canvas offers templates ("Automation", "Software", "Conversation"); parts can also be dragged from the left panel.
- Connect boxes by dragging from a dot on a box edge to another box; tap a box or an arrow to edit it in the right panel.
- "Export" (top right) > PNG, SVG or JSON opens the share sheet. "File" > "Import JSON" opens the Files picker.
- "Sign in" (top right) with the demo account; "Save" stores the diagram; "File" > "My projects" lists saved diagrams.
- Account deletion: account button (top right) > "Profile" > "Delete account" > confirm. The account and its diagrams are deleted immediately; you may test this with the demo account.
- iPhone: File, Export and account items are in the ☰ menu; the bottom bar has Add, undo/redo, edit, delete and fit.
- Turkish labels: Dosya = File, Projelerim = My projects, Dışa aktar = Export, Kaydet = Save, Giriş yap = Sign in, Profil = Profile, Hesabı sil = Delete account, Anlatarak çiz = Draw from a description.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- flowdraft's own backend at flowdraft.tech (self-hosted Supabase: auth and database) stores accounts and diagrams saved to an account. It is contacted only to sign up, sign in or save.
- Optional, user-initiated: "File" > "Connect AI tools" shows the address of flowdraft's MCP server. A user can add it to an AI assistant of their choice and authorize it with their flowdraft account; the assistant can then read and write that user's diagrams. The connection is made in the third-party tool and can be revoked in the same section of the app.
- Drawing from a description uses only Apple's on-device model.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking, payments or in-app purchases. Links to the terms, privacy policy, support page and guide open in a Safari view.
