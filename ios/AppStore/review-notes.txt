2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
flowdraft is a flowchart editor for people who plan processes: product and operations teams, developers and students sketching a workflow, an automation, a software architecture or a customer conversation. Users drag ready-made parts (steps, decisions, people, systems, notes, lanes) onto a canvas, connect them with arrows and export PNG, SVG or editable JSON. The editor runs entirely on the device from code bundled in the app, so it opens instantly and works offline; the drawing is kept on the device automatically. A free account is optional and only adds cloud saving.

3. HOW TO USE
No account is needed for drawing, importing or exporting. For the account features please use the demo account in the Sign-In Information fields.
- Launch the app. The empty canvas offers templates: tap "Automation", "Software" or "Conversation" to start from one, or drag parts from the left panel onto the canvas.
- Connect boxes by dragging from a dot on a box edge to another box. Tap a box or an arrow to edit its text, color and style in the right panel.
- Export: "Export" (top right) > "PNG image", "SVG vector" or "JSON (editable)". The share sheet opens (Save to Files, AirDrop, Photos, other apps).
- Import: "File" (top left) > "Import JSON" opens the Files picker for a previously exported .json file.
- Account (optional): "Sign in" (top right) > enter the demo email and password > "Sign in". Then "Save" stores the diagram in the account; "File" > "My projects" lists saved diagrams; the "Auto-save" switch turns on auto-save.
- Account deletion: when signed in, tap the round account button (top right, the email's first letter) > "Profile" > "Delete account" > confirm. The account and all its saved diagrams are deleted immediately and permanently. You may test this with the demo account.
- iPhone (portrait, compact layout): File, Export, Sign in and account items are in the ☰ menu (top right); the bottom bar has Add, undo/redo, edit panel, delete and fit.
- Turkish labels: Dosya = File, Projelerim = My projects, Dışa aktar = Export, Kaydet = Save, Giriş yap = Sign in, Profil = Profile, Hesabı sil = Delete account, Otomatik kaydet = Auto-save, Otomasyon = Automation.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- flowdraft's own backend at flowdraft.tech (self-hosted Supabase: auth and database) stores accounts (email, password hash) and diagrams saved to an account. It is only contacted to sign up, sign in or save. Diagrams are JSON; images and files cannot be uploaded.
- Optional, user-initiated: under "File" > "Connect AI tools" the app shows the address of flowdraft's MCP server and a link to a setup guide. A user can add this address to an AI assistant of their choice (any MCP client) and authorize it with their flowdraft account; the assistant can then read and write that user's diagrams. The connection is made in the third-party tool, not in this app, and can be revoked in the same section of the app.
- There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, payment processor or in-app purchase. Links to the terms, privacy policy, support page and guide open in a Safari view.
